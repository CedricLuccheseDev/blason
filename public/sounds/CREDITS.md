# Sounds

The game's sounds, every one free to share, so that they may live in this
public repository: under CC0, or under CC BY 4.0, whose authors are named
here as that license asks. The sounds come from [Freesound](https://freesound.org),
the music from Kevin MacLeod.
Each sound was cut to a short hit (the end of the game a little longer) or,
for the beds of the land, into a loop that plays on without a seam, faded and
brought to the same loudness, as the music was, and kept in Opus (an Ogg
file, a third lighter than MP3, and a loop without the blank MP3 lays at its
head); the cut is ours, the recording its author's. Where a family has several takes, the game turns
between them, never the same twice running (`src/audio/cues.ts`, `beds.ts`).
