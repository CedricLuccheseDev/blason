## D-CVI-1: Three coups that can turn a game

- Status: Accepted
- Owner: Cédric
- Updated: 2026-10-29
- Links: P1
#### Why?
- Need: a way back for the behind, a spend for gold late in the game.
- Evidence: OpenFront's bombs turn games; each of ours is a single deed.
#### What?
- Archers (kill troops), the troublemaker (land turned free, held by rebels),
  the trebuchet (works destroyed, far); each costs twice with each launched,
  eight times at most, and is seen coming.
#### Validation
- Confidence: Preliminary.

## D-CVI-2: The race, a share that falls with time

- Status: Accepted
- Owner: Cédric
- Updated: 2026-10-28
#### Why?
- Need: games that end, around twenty minutes.
#### What?
- A lord (or team) wins holding the share shown in the race: it starts high
  and drops 5 points a minute down to 50 %; the last side standing wins too.
- Acceptance: Given the arena's 24 normal bots, When a game is played out,
  Then it ends by share between 15 and 30 minutes.
#### Validation
- Evidence: arena 30/10, 19–22 minutes.
- Confidence: Supported.
